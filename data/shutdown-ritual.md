# End-of-day shutdown (~3 minutes)

1. **Log** — Record today's hours and time slots (done for you if you told Cursor).
2. **Pick one** — Write tomorrow's first task only (not the whole backlog).
3. **Schedule** — Block exact hours you'll work tomorrow, around meetings.
4. **Close** — Quit Cursor, close work tabs, step away from the desk.
5. **Release** — Say: *"Work is parked until tomorrow."*

The handoff is the point — so your brain can stop rehearsing tickets overnight.
